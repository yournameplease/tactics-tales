## Description

Amortized recruitment quota system. Credits accumulate at a configurable rate per battle (default ~1.5). Quota is spent on the opportunity, not the outcome. A configurable window (default 2 battles) forces a recruitment if the quota is unspent. Supports post-battle and mid-battle recruitment forms with player prompts.
